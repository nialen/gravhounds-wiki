<h2 id="answer">Is GRAVHOUNDS planned for Game Pass?</h2>

**Games media report that GRAVHOUNDS is planned to launch through Game Pass.** Gematsu says it will be available via the service, while TheSixthAxis describes day-one Xbox Game Pass availability. Both reports were published during the August 25, 2026 announcement cycle.

<StatusCallout title="Reported by multiple outlets">

The reviewed Xbox product page did not display a direct day-one Game Pass badge in the captured text. Treat the subscription launch as well-supported reporting that still needs a final store or official Game Pass listing close to release.

</StatusCallout>

<h2 id="confidence">How strong is the evidence?</h2>

Two named media outlets carry the claim, so it is stronger than a single rumor or community post. However, the reports appeared on the same announcement day and likely draw from the same press-release information. They are not fully independent confirmation of an unchanged launch contract.

That distinction matters because service plans can change and because a generic Game Pass statement can omit regional or subscription details. This page records what has been reported without claiming that every subscriber on every tier will have access.

<h2 id="unknown">Which details remain unconfirmed?</h2>

The reviewed sources do not clearly establish:

- the exact Game Pass tier or tiers that will include the game;
- whether PC and console catalog timing will be identical;
- regional availability;
- how Early Access packaging interacts with the subscription;
- whether access continues through the eventual full release.

The separate [platforms guide](/en/platforms/) covers confirmed Windows PC and Xbox Series X|S availability. The [release date guide](/en/release-date/) explains why November 2, 2026 remains a reported date while Steam still says Coming soon.

The safest pre-release action is to follow the official Xbox product page and Game Pass announcements. No purchase or subscription decision should rely only on an undated search snippet or repeated social post.
