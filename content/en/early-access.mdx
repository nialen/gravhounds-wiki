<h2 id="why">Why is GRAVHOUNDS entering Early Access?</h2>

Octopus Panic says Early Access will let the team build the game with community feedback while expanding its systems and content. The Steam questionnaire describes the core gameplay loop as playable and externally tested, but not as the finished form of the game.

That makes Early Access a development phase with a usable foundation. It should not be read as a guarantee that every announced idea will arrive on a fixed schedule.

<h2 id="launch-scope">What is expected to be playable?</h2>

The official description says players can take jobs, explore worlds, gather resources, build and defend infrastructure, fight alien threats and return from an operation. This is the same high-level gather, build, defend and extract loop described on the wider Steam page.

The questionnaire does not provide a complete launch count for worlds, enemies, weapons or characters. It also does not establish finished balance, progression pacing or endgame content.

<h2 id="plans">What does the developer plan to add?</h2>

Steam lists a direction that includes more Gravhounds, jobs, worlds, enemies, weapons, upgrades and progression. The developer also expects to improve the experience through feedback and continued development.

<StatusCallout title="A plan is not a fixed roadmap">

These categories describe intended expansion. They do not include guaranteed counts, dates or a locked order of delivery. This guide uses words such as plans and expects for that reason.

</StatusCallout>

Players looking for the systems already described can use the [gameplay overview](/en/gameplay/). Detailed character skills and progression builds remain unpublished because current official text does not support them.

<h2 id="duration">How long could Early Access last?</h2>

The developer estimates **around six months or longer**. The open-ended wording is important: development feedback and scope can extend that period. There is no official full-release calendar date in the reviewed questionnaire.

Steam also says the price is expected to increase when the game leaves Early Access. No exact Early Access price or full-release price is confirmed in the reviewed source. See the [release status page](/en/release-date/) for the separate conflict between Steam's Coming soon label and the November 2, 2026 date reported by media.
