<h2 id="confirmed">Which platforms are confirmed?</h2>

GRAVHOUNDS is confirmed for **Windows PC and Xbox Series X|S**. PC players have an official Steam page, and the Xbox announcement coverage also identifies a Microsoft Store PC release. Xbox has an official product listing optimized for Series X|S.

<h2 id="features">What do the stores confirm?</h2>

Steam lists single-player, online co-op and controller support. The game description supports teams of one to four, while the Xbox feature panel separates single-player from two to four player online cooperation.

The Xbox listing includes Xbox Play Anywhere. That feature generally connects an eligible Xbox and Windows purchase within the Microsoft ecosystem, but the exact release terms should be checked on the live product page. The store also displays 4K, HDR10 and 60 fps or higher feature labels for the Xbox version.

Those console labels do not guarantee a specific mode on every PC. Likewise, Steam controller support does not by itself establish feature parity with the Xbox build.

<h2 id="not-confirmed">Which platforms are not confirmed?</h2>

No reviewed official source announces PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, macOS or Linux versions. The Xbox page contains some broad compatibility wording, but the clear optimization target is Xbox Series X|S. This guide does not convert that wording into a claim of a native Xbox One release.

<StatusCallout title="Steam and Xbox cross-network play is unconfirmed">

The Xbox listing includes cross-platform labels within the Xbox ecosystem, but it does not explicitly state that Steam players can join Xbox players. A dedicated crossplay page remains unpublished until an official source answers that question.

</StatusCallout>
