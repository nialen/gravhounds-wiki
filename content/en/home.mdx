## A field manual built around evidence

GRAVHOUNDS is a cooperative action builder from Octopus Panic, Inc. Official store descriptions place one to four players on hostile planets where a crew gathers resources, constructs industrial systems, defends the job site and leaves before an escalating swarm overwhelms the operation.

This site separates confirmed platform information from media reports and early community discussion. That matters while the game is still in pre-release. Store details can change, announced plans can move and promotional footage does not provide enough evidence for precise tactics.
