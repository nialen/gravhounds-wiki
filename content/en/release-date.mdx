<h2 id="answer">What is the GRAVHOUNDS release date?</h2>

The most defensible answer is that **November 2, 2026 is a reported Early Access date, while Steam still lists GRAVHOUNDS as Coming soon**. The date appeared in games media coverage on August 25, 2026. It was not displayed on the official Steam store page when this guide was checked on August 26.

<StatusCallout title="Reported date, not store-confirmed">

Treat November 2 as the current announced target reported by media, not as an irreversible final date. The official store status has higher authority for purchase and download availability.

</StatusCallout>

<h2 id="meaning">Will this be the full release?</h2>

No. The developer's Steam questionnaire says the first public commercial version is intended for **Early Access**. The team expects to continue adding content and improving the game after that point. The current estimate for the Early Access period is around six months or longer, but that is a development expectation, not a guaranteed full-release date.

Read the [Early Access guide](/en/early-access/) for the planned scope and the language Octopus Panic uses around future content.

<h2 id="watch">What should players watch next?</h2>

The highest-value confirmation will be a dated update on Steam or the official GRAVHOUNDS site. A live purchase or install button would settle availability more strongly than a repeated news summary. Until then, wishlist the game on Steam, use the official playtest request if it is visible, and recheck platform listings close to November 2.

There is no confirmed price in the reviewed material. The Steam Early Access note says the developer expects the price to increase when leaving Early Access, but it does not establish a launch price.
