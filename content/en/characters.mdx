<h2 id="confirmed">What is officially confirmed?</h2>

Official descriptions present the playable crew as bio-engineered canine workers called Gravhounds. They are equipped for dangerous industrial jobs on alien planets and use tools, weapons and gravity manipulation while building and defending an operation.

Steam refers to distinct or unique Gravhounds, which supports the idea of character variety. The reviewed official store text does not provide a written roster, role list, ability table or voice cast.

<h2 id="names">The currently reported five-character roster</h2>

mxdwn Games names five playable characters:

- Zane
- Dozer
- Selene
- Finn
- Luna

<StatusCallout title="Single-source roster">

These five names currently rely on one accessible media article. They have not been matched to an official written roster in the reviewed Steam, Xbox or official-site text. Treat spellings and final availability as provisional.

</StatusCallout>

The media report says the characters have individual abilities, but it does not provide enough verified detail to publish ability descriptions. This page deliberately avoids assigning a tank, support, damage or engineering class based on visual appearance.

<h2 id="unknown">What information is still missing?</h2>

No reviewed source reliably establishes:

- each character's abilities, statistics or starting equipment;
- whether every character is available at the beginning;
- progression trees or build choices;
- biographies, relationships or voice actors;
- whether additional Gravhounds arrive during Early Access.

The Steam Early Access questionnaire says the developer plans more Gravhounds over time, but that is a direction rather than a promised count.
